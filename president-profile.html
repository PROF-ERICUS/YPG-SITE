PRESIDENT, YOUTH PARLIAMENT GHANA

OSMAN ABDUL-LATIF-CHEBO

President, Youth Parliament Ghana

The President provides leadership and direction for Youth Parliament Ghana, working with members and leadership structures to support meaningful youth participation and representation.

The Office of the President serves as a central leadership point for coordinating the work of the Youth Parliament and promoting constructive engagement among its members.

Leadership Responsibilities

Key areas associated with the leadership role of the President of Youth Parliament Ghana.

Leadership

Providing direction and leadership to the Youth Parliament and supporting the work of its members and leadership structures.

Coordination

Supporting coordination among the different structures and activities of Youth Parliament Ghana.

Youth Engagement

Encouraging constructive participation and dialogue among young people on issues relevant to leadership, representation and civic responsibility.
