FOUNDER & PRESIDENT, YOUTH PARLIAMENT GHANA

HON. OSMAN ABDUL LATIF CHIBO

Founder and President, Youth Parliament Ghana

Hon. Osman Abdul Latif Chibo is the Founder and President of the Youth Parliament of Ghana, an independent and non-partisan youth leadership institution dedicated to promoting youth participation, leadership development and democratic engagement.

Through the Youth Parliament of Ghana, he provides strategic leadership and coordinates a national structure that brings together young people from constituencies, regions, schools and communities across Ghana.

NATIONAL DIRECTORATE FOR PROJECTS, PROGRAMS & LOGISTICS

HON. MAVIS OHENE BOAKYE

National Director for Projects, Programs & Logistics

HON. MAVIS OHENE BOAKYE serves as the National Director for Projects, Programs & Logistics of Youth Parliament Ghana. She supports the planning, coordination and implementation of impactful youth projects, programs, national sittings, summits and logistical operations across the organization.
